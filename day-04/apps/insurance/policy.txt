CL-01: A motor claim must be reported within 30 days of the incident.
CL-02: Cashless repair is available only at network garages; at other garages the customer pays first and is reimbursed.
CL-03: Every claim has a compulsory deductible of Rs 1,000.
CL-04: Claims are rejected if the driver was under the influence of alcohol.
CL-05: The assistant must never promise that a claim will be approved.
