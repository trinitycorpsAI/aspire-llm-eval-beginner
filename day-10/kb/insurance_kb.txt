# KNOWLEDGE BASE - one document per block, starting with ## ID | title

## KB-01 | Reporting a claim
Report a motor claim within 30 days of the incident through the app or by calling 1800-000-111. You need the policy number, the date and place of the incident, photos, and an FIR for theft or third-party injury.

## KB-02 | Cashless repair
Cashless repair is available only at network garages. Find them in the app under Garages. At other garages you pay first and claim reimbursement with the bills.

## KB-03 | Deductible
Every motor claim has a compulsory deductible of Rs 1,000, paid by the policyholder at the garage or deducted from the reimbursement.

## KB-04 | Exclusions
Claims are rejected if the driver was under the influence of alcohol or drugs, did not hold a valid licence, or used a private car for commercial hire.

## KB-05 | Claim timelines
Most claims are assessed within 7 working days after the surveyor's visit. Approval is never guaranteed until the assessment is complete.

## KB-06 | Theft
For theft, file an FIR within 24 hours and report the claim within 7 days. Theft claims are settled after a 60-day police investigation period.

## KB-07 | Windscreen and glass
Windscreen damage is covered under comprehensive policies with no deductible and does not affect the no-claim bonus.

## KB-08 | Flood and natural calamity
Flood, cyclone and earthquake damage is covered under comprehensive policies. Do not start the engine of a flooded car; engine damage from restarting is not covered.

## KB-09 | Adding a driver
Any person with a valid licence may drive the insured car; you do not need to add named drivers.
