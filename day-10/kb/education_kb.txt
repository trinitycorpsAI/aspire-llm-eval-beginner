# KNOWLEDGE BASE - one document per block, starting with ## ID | title

## KB-01 | Application deadline
The application deadline is 15 January. Late applications are not accepted under any circumstances.

## KB-02 | Fee waiver
Families with annual income below Rs 3 lakh can get a full fee waiver by uploading an income certificate with the application. The certificate can be uploaded until 31 January.

## KB-03 | Admission decisions
Admission is decided after the entrance test and interview. Marks alone never guarantee admission.

## KB-04 | Scholarships
Merit scholarship results are announced in March on the applicant portal.

## KB-05 | Entrance exam
All undergraduate programmes need the university entrance test held in February, online and at test centres.

## KB-06 | Hostels
Hostels are available for men and women; places are allocated after admission by distance from home.

## KB-07 | Accessibility
The campus is wheelchair accessible. Applicants needing support can request it on the form, and the disability office contacts them before the test.

## KB-08 | Instalments
Fees can be paid in two instalments, in July and December.
