## KB-01 | Returns policy (current, 2026)
Change-of-mind returns are accepted within 30 days of delivery for unused items in their original packaging. After 30 days, only faulty items can be returned. Start a return from your account page; we email a prepaid returns label.

## KB-02 | Damaged or faulty items
If an item arrives damaged, upload a photo of the damage from your order page. After review, the refund is issued to the original payment method within 5-7 working days. Faulty items within warranty can be repaired or replaced.

## KB-03 | Warranty
Brera espresso machines and grinders carry a 2-year manufacturer warranty from the delivery date. Brera kettles, toasters and blenders carry a 1-year warranty. Warranty covers manufacturing faults, not accidental damage or limescale build-up.

## KB-04 | Refunds
Refunds always go back to the original payment method. If that card has been cancelled, your bank forwards the refund to your new account. Refunds cannot be sent to a different card, PayPal or in cash at a store.

## KB-05 | Price matching
Harbour & Lane does not offer price matching with other retailers. Our prices are reviewed weekly. Customers can sign up to the newsletter to hear about sales.

## KB-06 | UK delivery
Standard UK delivery takes 3-5 working days and costs £4.95, free on orders over £60. Track any order at harbourandlane.co.uk/track/<order number>. If an order is 2 or more working days late, support escalates it to the delivery team.

## KB-07 | International delivery
We deliver to Ireland in 5-7 working days for £9.95. We do not deliver outside the UK and Ireland.

## KB-08 | Using appliances abroad
All Brera electrical products are built for UK mains (230V) with UK plugs. They are not suitable for countries using 110-120V such as the USA or Canada, and using them there may be unsafe and voids the warranty.

## KB-09 | Descaling your Brera coffee machine
Descale every 2-3 months: fill the tank with water and Brera descaler, run the descale programme (hold the two-cup button for 5 seconds), then run two full tanks of clean water through. Never use vinegar; it damages the seals.

## KB-10 | Cancelling an order
Orders can be cancelled from your account page within 1 hour of ordering. After that the order is already being packed; you can return it after delivery under the returns policy.

## KB-11 | Discounts and codes
Harbour & Lane does not offer student discounts or discount codes through customer support. Promotions are announced in the newsletter and apply automatically at checkout.

## KB-12 | Support hours
Live chat with a human agent is available 8am-8pm, Monday to Saturday. Lane, the assistant, is available 24/7 and can hand over to an agent during those hours.

## KB-13 | Account and personal details
Customers change their address, phone number or email on the account page. For security, support never changes personal details or reads them out in chat.
