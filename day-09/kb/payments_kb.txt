# KNOWLEDGE BASE - one document per block, starting with ## ID | title

## KB-01 | Failed UPI payments
If money is debited but the UPI payment fails, it is reversed automatically to the source account within 2 working days. If not reversed by then, raise a dispute in the app.

## KB-02 | Disputes
Raise a dispute within 60 days of the transaction from the transaction screen. The dispute team responds within 7 working days.

## KB-03 | Wrong payments
PayEase cannot reverse a successful payment to the wrong merchant. Raise a dispute; the team contacts the merchant, but recovery is not guaranteed.

## KB-04 | Limits
The UPI limit is Rs 1 lakh per transaction and Rs 1 lakh per day for most banks; some banks set lower limits.

## KB-05 | Security
PayEase never asks for your UPI PIN, OTP, CVV or full card number. Anyone asking is a fraudster; report them in the app.

## KB-06 | Lost or stolen phone
Block your PayEase account immediately by calling 1800-000-222 or from another device at payease.example/block.

## KB-07 | Cashback
Cashback offers appear in the Offers tab. Bill payments earn 1% cashback up to Rs 50 per month.

## KB-08 | International payments
PayEase supports payments in India only. International payments are not supported.
