# KNOWLEDGE BASE - one document per block, starting with ## ID | title

## KB-01 | Health claims
Ads must never say a product cures, prevents or treats a medical condition. Allowed: 'helps reduce dust and pollen indoors' for air purifiers.

## KB-02 | Discounts
Discounts must match the brief exactly and every discount ad must include 'T&Cs apply'.

## KB-03 | Competitors
Ads must not mention competitor brands or compare prices with them by name.

## KB-04 | Length
Headlines must be 90 characters or fewer; body copy 200 characters or fewer.

## KB-05 | Air purifier facts
Our air purifiers have a HEPA H13 filter that captures 99.97% of particles of 0.3 microns. Filter replacement every 12 months.

## KB-06 | Smart TV facts
Our 55-inch smart TV has 4K resolution, Dolby Vision and a 2-year warranty.

## KB-07 | Languages
Ads may be written in English, Hindi, Tamil or Punjabi; regional ads use the same rules.

## KB-08 | Superlatives
Words like 'best' or 'No. 1' need a cited source in the brief; otherwise do not use them.

## KB-90 | Brand guidelines (ARCHIVED 2023)
ARCHIVED: 'T&Cs apply' optional for discounts under 20%. Replaced in 2025.
